About

Hi, I'm Vashisht.

I spend my weekdays inside deals, pulling apart companies' numbers to figure out what's really going on underneath. Before that, I spent four years in supply chain consulting, moving boxes around the world on spreadsheets and occasionally in real life. Somewhere along the way I realized the thing I actually love is systems: how they work, where they break, and what happens when you rebuild them a little better.

That curiosity leaks everywhere. I build small software tools for problems that annoy me: an app for two, an automation that saves my team hours, half-finished trading experiments. I'm teaching myself to DJ, which turns out to be another systems problem, just one you can dance to. And I fly a lot, mostly to eat things and get lost in new cities.

This site is my corner of the internet: what I'm building, what I'm learning, and what's currently on repeat.
