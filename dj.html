DJ Journey

The booth

A few weeks ago, I decided to learn to DJ. Instead of signing up for lessons, I asked AI to build me a 12-week curriculum tailored to my controller, my music taste, and my schedule. Then I built a practice tracker to keep myself honest.

This page is the journal of a complete beginner. Every practice session, every breakthrough, and every trainwreck transition is part of the story.

Goal: a 30-minute recorded mix, published by end of August 2026.

The AI-built curriculum

Twelve weeks, three phases

Designed with AI, tracked in a small app I built for it, adjusted as reality intervenes. Tap a phase for the summary, or open the full plan for every session and drill.

View the full 12-week plan

    • Week 1, Know Your Weapon: learn the DDJ-FLX4 layout until my hands move without thinking. No mixing yet.
    • Week 2, The 3-Step System: a repeatable routine for every single mix, so I never freeze up.
    • Week 3, EQ Mixing: blend tracks by cutting bass on one and boosting the other. The technique that makes mixes sound clean.

    • Week 4, Hip-Hop Deep Dive: slower BPMs, more space between beats, bigger drops.

    • Week 6, The Genre Switch: moving from hip-hop to house without it sounding like a car changing lanes without signaling. The signature move.

Why an AI coach

Feedback loops. A weekly lesson gives you one correction per week; describing a botched transition to an AI coach gets me a targeted drill for tomorrow's session. It's not better than a great human teacher. It's better than no teacher, and it's available at 11pm when I actually practice.

Practice tool

The virtual deck

I didn't build this because I wanted another controller. I built it because I wanted a better way to learn. When I watched DJ sets, I often knew what sounded good, but not how it happened. The hands moved too fast, and it was easy to miss the small adjustments that made the mix work. This browser-based controller lets me slow things down, visualize every knob, fader, and pad movement, and build the same muscle memory through repetition. Connect a DDJ-FLX4 or FLX2 over USB, and it mirrors every input live.

It started as a tool for me, but it also makes a surprisingly good introduction for anyone who's curious about DJing before buying a controller.
