DJ Journey · The Plan

Twelve weeks, in full

This is the complete curriculum an AI coach and I designed together: 12 weeks, 3 sessions per week, every session a structured hour of drills built for the DDJ-FLX4. Published here as a reference, and as a commitment device. Tap any week to see the session-by-session plan.

Three sessions a week, one hour each. Mix one records in week 9, publishes in week 10.

Build Your System

  1. The Layout Drill

    Map every knob, button, and fader by feel

    0:00-0:10 Power On & Load Up

    Open Rekordbox. Load 'Sicko Mode' (Travis Scott) on Deck 1 and 'God's Plan' (Drake) on Deck 2. Get comfortable pressing play and pause on both decks.

    Tracks: Sicko Mode / God's Plan
    0:10-0:25 The Fader Walk

    With both tracks playing, slowly move the crossfader left to right. Feel how the sound shifts. Do this 20 times, no rushing. Notice how volume changes before the sound fully switches.

    Tracks: Any Drake or Travis track
    0:25-0:40 Channel Gain & EQ

    On Deck 1: turn the HIGH knob all the way down and hear what disappears (treble, hi-hats). Turn LOW all the way down (bass, kick). Repeat on Deck 2. Learn what each knob kills.

    Tracks: Sicko Mode
    0:40-0:55 The Loop Button

    With Deck 1 playing, press the 4-beat AUTO loop. Let it loop, press again to exit. 10 times on the same track.

    Tracks: Highest in the Room
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Write one thing that surprised you about the controller today.

    Cue Points

    Set and hit cue points perfectly

    0:00-0:10 What Is a Cue Point?

    A cue point is a bookmark in a track. Load 'HUMBLE.' by Kendrick, find the moment the beat drops, press CUE there.

    Tracks: HUMBLE.
    0:10-0:30 Hot Cue Drill

    Set 3 hot cues: 1 at the very start, 2 at the drop, 3 at the breakdown. Hit them in order, 1-2-3, over and over for 20 minutes.

    Tracks: HUMBLE.
    0:30-0:50 New Track, Same Drill

    Load 'Carbon' by Drake, set the same 3 hot cues, practice jumping between them.

    Tracks: Carbon
    0:50-1:00 Log It

    Which cue was hardest to hit consistently?

    Tempo & BPM

    Understand BPM and the pitch fader

    0:00-0:15 What Is BPM?

    Beats per minute. Hip-hop sits around 85-100, house around 125-130. Load 'Nonstop' (Drake), watch the BPM display, tap your finger to the beat and count.

    Tracks: Nonstop
    0:15-0:35 The Pitch Fader

    Push up for faster, down for slower. Try matching a Fred again track's BPM to a Drake track.

    Tracks: Jungle / Nonstop
    0:35-0:55 Sync Button Experiment

    Press SYNC on Deck 2 while Deck 1 plays and watch the BPM match automatically. Then turn sync off and try manually.

    Tracks: Any
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Does manual or sync feel more natural right now?

  2. Step 1 · Prepare

    Always know what's coming next

    0:00-0:15 The Queue Habit

    Before pressing play, load the next track. Rule: when a track starts playing, immediately load the next one on the other deck.

    Tracks: Any 5 Drake or Travis tracks
    0:15-0:35 Pre-listen (Headphone Cue)

    Plug in headphones, press CUE on the channel that is not playing live. You hear the next track in your headphones only.

    Tracks: God's Plan / Sicko Mode
    0:35-0:55 Find the Drop

    Every track has a drop, the moment it gets big. Find it in 5 tracks and set a hot cue on each.

    Tracks: Lane 8 / Fred again / Travis
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    How fast can you load a new track now versus day 1?

    Step 2 · Match

    Tempo matching without thinking

    0:00-0:20 Ear Training

    Load 'MAFIA' on Deck 1, tap your finger to every kick. Load 'Rumble' in headphones. Are they the same speed?

    Tracks: MAFIA / Rumble
    0:20-0:45 Pitch Fader Match Drill

    Use the pitch fader to match Deck 2 to Deck 1. Goal: within half a BPM without sync.

    Tracks: MAFIA / Rumble
    0:45-0:55 Try 3 Pairs

    Drake + Lane 8, Kendrick + Hugel, J. Cole + Fred again.

    Tracks: Various
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Which pair was hardest to match, and why?

    Step 3 · Transition

    Your first real mix

    0:00-0:15 The Simple Crossfade

    Track 1 playing, Track 2 ready and tempo matched. Wait for the end of a phrase (every 16 or 32 beats), then push the crossfader across over 4 beats. That's a mix.

    Tracks: God's Plan into Sicko Mode
    0:15-0:40 Do It 10 Times

    Load, match, crossfade. Again. Build the muscle memory.

    Tracks: Any Drake + Travis tracks
    0:40-0:55 Record One Attempt

    Hit record in Rekordbox, run your 3-step system once start to finish, play it back.

    Tracks: Any
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    What did you hear in your recording that surprised you?

  3. Bass Swap

    Never have two basses playing at once

    0:00-0:15 The Golden Rule

    Two bass lines at once equals mud. Play 'Laugh Now Cry Later' and 'Antidote' together, turn both LOW knobs to zero, and hear how empty it sounds.

    Tracks: Laugh Now Cry Later / Antidote
    0:15-0:40 The Bass Swap Drill

    Track 2 enters with LOW at zero. Crossfade it in, then slowly turn Track 2's LOW up as Track 1's LOW comes down.

    Tracks: Antidote / Carbon
    0:40-0:55 Repeat 5 Times

    Five bass swaps in a row. Focus on smoothness.

    Tracks: Any hip-hop or trap tracks
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Did you accidentally have two basses playing at once?

    High Cut Intro

    Bring tracks in with EQ, not volume

    0:00-0:20 The Ghost Intro

    Load the next track, turn HIGH and MID to zero, bring the channel fader up, then bring HIGH back in over 16 beats. You're sneaking a track in.

    Tracks: Lane 8, carbon copy
    0:20-0:45 Full EQ Transition

    Combine the bass swap and ghost intro: Track 2 starts with no HIGH and no LOW. Bring HIGH in first, then swap the bass.

    Tracks: Fred again / Hugel
    0:45-0:55 Record & Review

    Record one full EQ transition. Clean or muddy?
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Which EQ knob feels most natural now?

    Month 1 Review

    Put it all together: first 10-minute set

    0:00-0:10 Set Your Tracklist

    Pick 6 tracks: 2 hip-hop, then 2 trap, then 2 house or electronic. Write them down.

    Tracks: Your choice
    0:10-0:50 Record Your First Mini Set

    Hit record and play through all 6 using the 3-step system plus EQ mixing. Don't stop if it goes wrong.

    Tracks: Your 6 tracks
    0:50-1:00 Critical Listen

    Play it back. Note the best transition, the worst one, and one thing to fix next month.

Genre Switching

  1. Hip-Hop Phrasing

    Learn the 16-bar structure

    0:00-0:20 Count the Bars

    Load 'Rich Flex' and count 1-2-3-4 with the beat. Every 4 bars is a phrase, every 16 is a section. Transitions happen at the start of a 16-bar section.

    Tracks: Rich Flex
    0:20-0:45 Find the 16

    On 5 hip-hop tracks, identify where 16-bar sections start. Mark them as hot cues.

    Tracks: Kendrick, Drake, J. Cole
    0:45-0:55 On-Beat Entry

    Enter Track 2 exactly on beat 1 of a 16-bar section. Five attempts.
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    How many bars early do you need to start preparing?

    The Energy Arc

    Build energy across your hip-hop set

    0:00-0:15 Low to High Energy

    Start calm, build up. 'No Role Modelz' opens, 'HUMBLE.' is the middle, 'Sicko Mode' is the peak.

    Tracks: No Role Modelz / HUMBLE. / Sicko Mode
    0:15-0:50 Play the Arc

    Mix those 3 in order, three times. Make the energy feel like it's rising.

    Tracks: J. Cole into Kendrick into Travis
    0:50-1:00 Log It

    Where did the energy drop unexpectedly?

    Hip-Hop FX

    Echo and filter on hip-hop

    0:00-0:20 The Echo Out

    At the end of a track, hit ECHO and let the last beat ring out while Track 2 fades in. Five times.

    Tracks: Drake tracks
    0:20-0:45 The Filter Sweep

    Turn the filter knob slowly left over 8 beats until the track sounds distant, then bring Track 2 in clean.

    Tracks: Travis tracks
    0:45-0:55 Combine Both

    Echo out plus filter sweep equals a cinematic transition. Three times.
    0:55-1:00 Log It

    Which FX felt most natural?

    Use the energy of a drop to your advantage

    0:00-0:20 Anatomy of a Drop

    House FX

    Reverb, flanger, and build effects

    0:00-0:25 The Reverb Wash

    At the end of a house track, hit reverb and let the last chord wash out. Bring the new track in clean.

    Tracks: Lane 8, carbon copy
    0:25-0:50 The Flanger Build

    During a build, hit FLANGER for that whooshing tension. Cut it exactly when the drop hits.

    Tracks: Hugel / Fred again

  3. The BPM Bridge

    How to jump from 90 BPM to 128 BPM

    0:00-0:20 The Half-Time Trick

    Hip-hop at 90 BPM reads as half-speed house at 180. Slow a house track to about 95 to match Drake, then speed back up after the switch.

    Tracks: Drake / Fred again
    0:20-0:50 Slow Speed-Up Drill

    Hip-hop playing, house matched at 95, mix in, then pitch the house track up to 126 over 2 minutes.

    Tracks: God's Plan into Jungle
    0:50-1:00 Log It

    How fast can you change BPM without anyone noticing?
